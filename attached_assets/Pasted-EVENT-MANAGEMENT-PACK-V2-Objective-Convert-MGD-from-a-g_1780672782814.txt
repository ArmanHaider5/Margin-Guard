EVENT MANAGEMENT PACK V2

Objective:
Convert MGD from a generic warehouse diagnostic into an event-management operational intelligence engine.

====================================================
STEP 1 — EVENT SIGNAL ENGINE
============================

Create server/mgd/event-signals.ts

Generate the following metrics from extracted transactions and event-specific documents:

dispatchFailureRate
= dispatches marked incomplete / total dispatches

inventoryShortageRate
= missing items / total dispatched items

substitutionRate
= substitutions / total dispatched items

deliveryDelayRate
= delayed dispatches / total dispatches

averageDelayMinutes
= average delay mins

assetDamageRate
= damaged assets / active assets

damageRecoveryRate
= recovered damage value / total damage value

unrecoveredDamageValue
= total unrecovered charges

inventoryVisibilityScore
based on:

* adjustment frequency
* reconciliation frequency
* inventory discrepancy signals

eventReadinessScore
based on:

* dispatch completion
* substitutions
* missing items
* delay rates
