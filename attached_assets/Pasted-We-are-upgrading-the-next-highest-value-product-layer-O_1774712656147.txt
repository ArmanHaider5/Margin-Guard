We are upgrading the next highest-value product layer:

# OBJECTIVE

Make the **Recommended Next Actions** section more practical, execution-ready, and consulting-grade.

Right now the section is structurally good (This Week / 30-Day Stabilisation / 60–90 Day Structural Fixes), but each action still needs stronger business usefulness.

This task is to improve the **content and structure of each action card**, not redesign the whole page.

---

# PART A — BACKEND ACTION ENRICHMENT

## Goal

Every recommended action returned by the roadmap / actions engine should now include these additional fields:

```ts id="5v8pbd"
why?: string
expectedOutcome?: string
suggestedOwner?: string
priority?: "CRITICAL" | "HIGH" | "MEDIUM"
effort?: "LOW" | "MEDIUM" | "HIGH"
```

Do not remove or rename existing fields.

---

## RULES

### 1. why

A short explanation of why this action exists.

Examples:

* "Addresses recurring unplanned downtime and missing loss visibility."
* "Targets maintenance backlog accumulation and poor PM execution discipline."
* "Improves quality loss visibility and defect containment."

Must be deterministic and derived from the related finding / category / action type.

---

### 2. expectedOutcome

A short statement of the intended business result.

Examples:

* "Improves visibility into daily downtime loss."
* "Reduces hidden quality and rework cost."
* "Stabilises PM compliance for critical assets."

---

### 3. suggestedOwner

Assign a realistic business role based on category / action type.

Allowed examples:

* Maintenance Lead
* Production Manager
* Quality Supervisor
* Operations Manager
* Supply Chain Planner
* HR / Workforce Lead
* Finance Controller

Do not use names. Role only.

---

### 4. priority

Use deterministic logic:

* CRITICAL = actions in "This Week" OR directly tied to severe operational instability
* HIGH = actions in 30-Day Stabilisation
* MEDIUM = actions in 60–90 Day Structural Fixes

---

### 5. effort

Assign effort based on action complexity:

* LOW = tracking, review, workshop, visibility, audit
* MEDIUM = process rollout, planning cadence, controls, governance
* HIGH = system redesign, structural workflow changes, multi-team intervention
