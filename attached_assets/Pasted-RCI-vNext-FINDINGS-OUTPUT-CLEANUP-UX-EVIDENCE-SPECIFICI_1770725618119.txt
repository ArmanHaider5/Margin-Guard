RCI vNext – FINDINGS OUTPUT CLEANUP (UX + EVIDENCE SPECIFICITY)

Objective:
Make Findings outputs concrete, non-generic, and professionally structured so they are sellable to clients.

Instructions:

1. Enforce concrete Evidence Anchors:
   - Evidence anchors must reference:
     - documentName (file name)
     - specific signal (metric, anomaly, quote)
   - Do NOT allow generic anchors like:
     "uploaded operational documents"
     "pattern match"
     "money category detected"
   - If concrete anchors cannot be extracted, mark finding as EXPLORATORY and require user confirmation before display.

2. Collapse overlapping Money-category findings:
   - If multiple findings belong to Money and evidenceStrength is WEAK or MODERATE:
       - Keep 1 primary Money finding (highest evidenceStrength)
       - Suppress additional Money findings
   - Add a note: "Related financial impacts observed (collapsed)"

3. Redesign Findings UI into structured sections:
   Replace paragraph blocks with clear sections:
   - Finding Title
   - Category | Severity | Evidence Strength (badges)
   - What We Observed (Evidence Anchors - bullet points)
   - What This Indicates (Root Cause Hypothesis)
   - Why It Matters (Industry-specific lens)
   - Suggested Intervention Direction (bulleted)
   - Confidence & Next Evidence to Validate

4. Remove repetitive phrasing:
   - Eliminate:
     "There is a recurring pattern indicating..."
     "Why It Matters:" inline paragraph style
   - Replace with section headers + concise bullets

5. Update Diagnostic Confidence messaging:
   - Only show "Evidence insufficient" when evidenceStrength = WEAK
   - Do NOT show both "Evidence: Moderate" and "Diagnostic Confidence: Low" simultaneously
   - Align tone:
       STRONG → assertive
       MODERATE → hypothesis
       WEAK → exploratory

6. Do NOT change underlying scoring logic.
7. Do NOT break existing APIs or case workflows.
8. Keep backwards compatibility with existing Findings data.

Confirm once complete.
